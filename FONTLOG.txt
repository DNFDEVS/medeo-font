FONTLOG for Medeo
=================

Medeo is an open font family for Latin and Cyrillic: Medeo Sans, Medeo Mono, Medeo Pixel and, since 1.000,
Medeo Serif. Medeo Mono also comes as Medeo Mono Nerd Font for terminals. It is made by DNF Studio
(https://dnf.dev), which holds the copyright of its changes, for prtner.ai. Licensed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 (OFL.txt).

This log is about the fonts themselves: what they are made of and, version by version, what changed in their
glyphs, features, spacing and build. What a release changes for the people who use Medeo is in CHANGELOG.md, and
the languages each family supports, with their number, are in LANGUAGES.md.


What it is made of
------------------

Medeo Sans
- Outlines, metrics, most of the Latin and Cyrillic, figures and punctuation, and the upright a: Geist by Vercel
  and Basement Studio (Copyright 2024 and 2026 The Geist Project Authors, OFL 1.1). Some kerning pairs follow
  Geist's proportions since 1.000-rc4. The outlines of p and р are Geist's; since 1.000 their descender has
  Inter's depth.
- From Inter by Rasmus Andersson (Copyright 2016 The Inter Project Authors, OFL 1.1): the letters к з я ж К З Я Ж,
  л Л б (since 1.000, and with л Л also љ Љ ԓ Ԓ), the upright t, the italic e and t, ү and the bar of ұ Ұ, the bitcoin ₿, manat ₼, lari ₾ and lira ₺ signs, the
  check mark ✓ and black star ★, the descender depth of 1.000, the overall spacing tone of Latin and Cyrillic,
  and the change of spacing between Inter's text and display optical sizes, which the display end of Medeo's opsz
  axis follows.
- Drawn by DNF Studio: the tenge ₸, Uzbek ʻ, the narrow no-break, thin and figure spaces, the Black master narrowed
  as Inter builds heavy weights, the optical size axis (opsz 14–32) with its display kerning, and every spacing and
  kerning edit since. In 1.000-rc4: the Cyrillic of Bashkir, Chuvash, Udmurt, Mari, Sakha, Komi, Ossetian,
  Azerbaijani and Talysh, Altai, Khakas, Shor, Abkhaz and the languages of the North; the West African letters Ɔ Ɛ Ɓ
  Ɗ Ƒ Ɣ Ɩ Ʋ Ɲ Ɨ Ʉ Ǝ Ɖ Ƴ Ƙ Ʊ Ʌ ʔ; Ҧ ҧ; Serbian and Macedonian italic б г д п т; letters with a dot below, a caron, a
  breve or stacked marks; narrow marks for i, a dotless ɨ, compact marks for stacks on capitals, and the marks
  U+030D U+0313 U+0330 U+0331. They are built from the family's own strokes, bowls, hooks and tails; where no part
  existed (the loops of Ɣ ɣ, Ҩ ҩ), the part is drawn in the family's strokes. In 1.000: the heavy check mark ✔ (the
  ✓ with a heavier stroke), the Belarusian ruble sign (Б with a bar, built like ₽), the soft and non-breaking
  hyphens from the hyphen, and the zero-width space, non-joiner and joiner.

Medeo Mono
- Outlines and metrics: Geist Mono (Copyright 2024 and 2026 The Geist Project Authors, OFL 1.1).
- Drawn by DNF Studio: ₸ ₼ ₾ ₺ built from Mono's own letters in its cell, Uzbek ʻ, top anchors and language systems
  for Cyrillic stress marks. In 1.000-rc4: the same new Cyrillic, Abkhaz and West African letters as Medeo Sans,
  Ҧ ҧ, Serbian and Macedonian italic forms, fitted to the cell the way Mono draws Ж Ш М; the slanted Ʒ ʒ Ǯ ǯ of the
  italic; anchors and mark stacking for the new letters. In 1.000: г ѓ ґ ӷ built from the parts of Mono's own r, and
  the Belarusian ruble sign. PT Mono by ParaType served as the reference for the construction of г and ӷ and for the
  depth of the hooks; no outline is taken from it. Nothing is taken from Inter.

Medeo Mono Nerd Font
- Medeo Mono with the icons of Nerd Fonts 3.5.1 (font-patcher, complete icon set, one-cell icons). The icon sets
  keep their own licences, which travel with the fonts: Nerd Fonts, Codicons, Font Awesome, Material Design,
  Octicons, Pomicons, Powerline Symbols, Powerline Extra Symbols and Weather Icons.

Medeo Pixel
- The pixel grid, the pixel component and the five dot shapes of the ELSH axis: Geist Pixel (Copyright 2026 The
  Geist Project Authors, OFL 1.1).
- Drawn by DNF Studio on that grid: the Cyrillic of Russian, Ukrainian, Kazakh, Kyrgyz, Tajik and Uzbek, and the
  signs ₸ ₼ ₾ ₺ ₽. In 1.000-rc4: the Cyrillic of the languages added to Medeo Sans, the palochka Ӏ ӏ, Ѓ ѓ Ќ ќ, Ҧ ҧ,
  Abkhaz, the West African letters, Ŋ ŋ, the hook above, the horn and the dot below with the full Vietnamese set,
  Ŧ ŧ and Ǧ ǧ in all five element shapes, ₴ and ₹, and tabular figures. In 1.000: the Belarusian ruble sign and a
  new ₽. Nothing is taken from Inter.

Medeo Serif
- Latin, figures, punctuation, signs, metrics, kerning and features: Geist Serif Regular 1.001 by WORDMARK, Mark
  Davis and Andrés Briganti (Copyright 2026 The Geist Project Authors, OFL 1.1), as drawn. Geist Serif has one
  weight and no italic, and so does Medeo Serif. Its "Regular" is the round-dot style of Geist Serif. Its stylistic
  sets ss01–ss05 are renumbered to Medeo's scheme (see 1.000 below) and also switch the Cyrillic.
- The Cyrillic, which Geist Serif does not have, is built by DNF Studio from Geist Serif's own parts: stems and
  serifs measured from H and n, bowls from P, shoulders from E, the hook of J, the legs of K, Я from R, Ю from O, Э
  and э from mirrored C and c. Cyrillic letters with a Latin twin (А В Е М Н О Р С Т Х а е о р с у х and others) are
  that Latin letter; Ӏ ӏ are I, Ӕ ӕ are Æ æ. Lowercase letters without a twin are small capitals of the capitals,
  narrowed to the width of the Latin lowercase, with the hairlines and stems restored. The twins space and kern
  exactly as the Latin letters. The other Cyrillic letters take the sidebearings of a Latin model (н п и as l h i)
  plus the difference PT Serif has between the two. Their kerning gives every Cyrillic pair the white of its Latin
  model pair in Geist Serif (ко as ko, жа as xa), so the Cyrillic sets as tightly as Geist's Latin, and keeps serifs
  at least 25 units apart.
- From PT Serif by ParaType: Alexandra Korolkova, Olga Umpeleva, Vladimir Yefimov (Copyright (c) 2010 ParaType
  Ltd., OFL 1.1, Reserved Font Names "PT Sans", "PT Serif" and "ParaType"):
  - the outlines of Б б Д д Л л Ф ф З з, scaled to the proportions of Geist Serif, with hairlines thinned to Geist's
    and Geist's stems, crossbars and wedge serifs; the bowl of б and the neck and flag above it;
  - the outlines of Ҩ ҩ Ҽ ҽ Ӡ ӡ, with Geist's hairlines;
  - the construction of the letters of the smaller Cyrillic languages, built from Geist Serif's parts: tails as on
    Ц (Ҭ ҭ Ӷ ӷ Ӌ ӌ Ҵ ҵ), hanging descenders as on Џ (Ҙ ҙ Ҫ ҫ Ҿ ҿ), the hooks of Ђ ђ Ӈ ӈ Ԥ ԥ Ԓ ԓ Ӄ ӄ Ҕ ҕ, bars and
    shoulders (Ҝ ҝ Ҟ ҟ Ҡ ҡ Ҥ ҥ Ҹ ҹ);
  - the construction of the pound sign £.
- From Noto Serif Display (Copyright 2012 Google Inc., OFL 1.1): the model for Ʊ ʊ Ʋ ʋ, with strokes thickened to
  Geist's.
- Drawn by DNF Studio from Geist Serif's parts: the African and other extended Latin letters Ɔ ɔ Ǝ ǝ Ɛ ɛ Ʌ ʌ Ŧ ŧ Ɨ ɨ
  Ʉ ʉ Ŋ ŋ Ɲ ɲ Ƒ ƒ Ɖ ɖ Ɓ ɓ Ɗ ɗ Ƙ ƙ Ƴ ƴ Ɩ ɩ Ɣ ɣ ʔ ʻ; accented Latin and Cyrillic letters composed from the font's
  letters and marks; the marks U+030D U+0329 U+0330 U+0331 U+1DC4–U+1DC7; the signs ₸ ₽ № ₴ ₼ ₾ ₺ ₿ ¢ £ ^ and the
  Belarusian ruble sign, from Geist Serif's stems, hairlines, wedge serifs and bowls; the figure, thin and narrow
  no-break spaces, soft and non-breaking hyphens, zero-width space, non-joiner and joiner, and the line and
  paragraph separators, as in Medeo Sans; top and bottom anchors and mark stacking for every new letter. Two fixes
  to the anchors of Geist Serif's Latin: Ə had none, and the top anchors of b d đ þ sat inside the ascender; they
  now sit above it, as on l.


Versions
--------

1.100 (2026-11-10)
All families
- The som sign U+20C0, from the construction of the National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic (Unicode proposal L2/20-261): a
  monoline ring cut at about 45 degrees above a bar on the baseline. Drawn by DNF Studio; ring width follows the letter
  c of each weight, and the air between ring and bar does not close in light weights.
- One web font per style; the per-script subsets are no longer built.
Medeo Sans
- One kerning table for all scripts, as classes without per-pair exceptions, fitted to the 1.000 kerning at every corner
  of the design space (sources/MedeoSans-kerning.json, sources/MedeoSans-Italic-kerning.json, scripts/class_kerning.py).
- The pair ад is +41 in Regular and +56 in Italic (1.000: +15 and +31), so that а and д no longer meet at -4% tracking.
- ss12 sets д in the form of the capital Д (uni0434.ss12 in the TTF fonts), upright and italic.
Medeo Mono
- Added ₿ (the letter B of Mono with the bars of Medeo Sans), ✓ ✔ ★ (from Medeo Sans), ﬁ ﬂ (both letters in one cell).
- Box Drawing and Block Elements (U+2500–259F, all 160 glyphs): drawn in a frame centred 45 units below the middle of
  the line (hhea ascent 1005, descent -295); every point moves up by 45 units, so the frame is centred and the seam of
  the half blocks is at 355, the middle of the line. The ends of horizontal strokes and the outer edges of blocks half a
  cell wide or wider reach 24 units into the neighbouring cell (strokes ended at -5 and 605 before); the left edge of a
  progress bar made of the left blocks therefore steps by 24 units between the three-eighths and the half block. In the
  static fonts a short run of instructions (scripts/box_hints.py) puts the ends of the solid strokes and blocks on
  usWinAscent and usWinDescent, rounded to pixels, where the rasterizer reports no sub-pixel positioning (the Windows
  GDI), so that consoles which size the line by the Windows metrics draw rows without a gap. The same run also fires in
  other rasterizers that report no sub-pixel positioning, for example FreeType's monochrome rendering, where the ends
  then overshoot a line of the standard height by a pixel or two. The dashed lines, the diagonals and the shades move
  with the rest and are not extended. Medeo Mono Nerd Font Mono carries the same outlines and instructions. Upright and
  italic, every weight.
Medeo Mono Nerd Font
- The new signs of Medeo Mono: ₿ ✓ ✔ ★ ﬁ ﬂ. The box drawing and blocks are described under Medeo Mono.
Medeo Pixel
- Added ₿ ฿ ₱ (its own B and P with bars), ₪, superscript and subscript figures, fractions, Ѐ Ѝ ѐ ѝ.
Medeo Serif
- Added ± ¬ superscript and subscript figures, fractions, ‰ ′ ″ ≈ ≠ ≤ ≥ ∞ √ µ, ₱ ฿ (its own P and B with bars), arrows
  from its own right arrow, Ѐ Ѝ ѐ ѝ, ﬁ ﬂ. ₹ and ₪ come from Medeo Sans.
- The pairs ьт ът ьъ ъъ are -80 (1.000: -140, -140, -135, -135). The slash keeps clear white to its neighbours (in 1.000
  eleven letters of Basic Latin and Cyrillic after it, and Q before it, touched it); the pair /у moves from 0 to 95.

1.000 (2026-10-31)
All families
- Variable fonts of Sans and Mono ship without TrueType hinting, like Inter, Geist and JetBrains Mono; the static
  fonts keep ttfautohint for Windows apps.
- meta table: dlng and slng are "Latn, Cyrl" in every font (Sans, Mono and Nerd had slng "Cyr", Pixel "Latn",
  Serif had no meta table).
- Smaller files: the static fonts of Sans, Sans Display and Mono are cut from the variable fonts with overlaps
  removed and the other components kept, then hinted with ttfautohint; Medeo Sans stores its kerning as classes plus
  exceptions in static, variable and web fonts, with every pair value kept (static Sans Regular 658 -> 263 KB,
  variable Sans 1091 -> 688 KB).
Medeo Serif
- New family, one style: Regular. Geist Serif Latin as drawn, with Cyrillic, extended Latin, signs and anchors
  added as described above. 759 languages (699 Latin, 60 Cyrillic), as Sans and Mono.
- Latin outlines, spacing and kerning are Geist Serif's: every Latin pair positions as in Geist Serif.
- Signs added: ₴ ₼ ₾ ₺ ₿, the Belarusian ruble sign at U+20C5 (the code Unicode 19.0 proposes), and ¢ £ ^, which
  Geist Serif does not have. The crossbar of ₽ is raised to the height PT Serif uses, clear of the leg's serif.
- Spaces and controls as in Medeo Sans: U+2007 U+2009 U+202F U+00AD U+2011 U+200B U+200C U+200D U+2028 U+2029.
- Stylistic sets in Medeo's numbering instead of Geist Serif's ss01–ss05: ss01 tailed a, ss04 alternate R, ss12
  alternate k, ss13 square dots, ss14 triangle dots, ss15 heart dots, ss16 round O Q o 0. The Cyrillic switches
  with the Latin (а ӑ ӓ in ss01; dotted letters in ss13–ss15; О о Ө ө Ю ю in ss16); alternates keep the marks and
  kerning of their base letter.
- К к Ж ж, and Қ қ Ҝ ҝ Ҟ ҟ Ҡ ҡ Ӄ ӄ Ќ ќ Җ җ Ӝ ӝ built on them: the arm and leg start from a short hairline bar
  at mid-height, as in Medeo Sans and PT Serif, instead of meeting near the stem. К keeps the width, serifs and
  kerning of K; Ҝ ҝ take the vertical stroke across the bar.
- Cyrillic spacing: 58 glyphs re-spaced; lowercase letters with stems sit like Latin l h i.
- Cyrillic kerning at Geist's density: every Cyrillic pair takes the white of its Latin model pair in Geist Serif
  (ко as ko, от as oz), which closes the gaps around о; serifs stay at least 25 units apart, and the top beak of
  л Л keeps 40. Pairs of Latin twins are equal to the Latin pairs (ро = po, сс = cc, ТА = TA).
- Cyrillic pairs that stood looser than the rest closed up (о–д, о–б, ё–р, о–с and others, 1508 pairs): every
  pair keeps its place or comes closer, none opens up; tight pairs, sidebearings and the Latin are unchanged. Pairs
  where one letter reaches over the other (Г Т У over lowercase, the tail of у) keep their place; no pair comes
  closer than 60 units in any direction, or than it stood before.
- Outline fixes: the stems of h ħ ђ straightened; б redrawn with a smooth bowl, without the hairline slits at its
  joins; the sliver in Ӈ and the extra dot of ǐ removed; coincident points removed, five contours turned the right way.
- Serif tips of the Cyrillic lowercase are 4 units tall, as in the Latin lowercase (they were 13: the emboldening
  that restores hairlines in letters drawn from capitals also thickened the tips).
- The bowl of Б aligned with its stem.
- Ӄ ӄ: the leg of К к bends into a round hook to the descender (-200) that thins to a hairline terminal cut at
  an angle, as in PT Serif (it was a J hook under the leg, to -150). Their widths are those of К к.
- Ҕ ҕ: a bowl under the bar of Г г runs down into a round hook (Ҕ to -150, ҕ to -200), as in PT Serif and Medeo
  Sans; ҕ was г with the shoulder of n and read like ŋ.
- Ҙ ҙ: the tail hangs from the lowest point of the bowl; its top no longer shows inside the counter.
- Saw-tooth points removed from the joins of З з Ɛ ɛ Ӡ ӡ Ҩ ҩ Ҽ ҽ Ҿ ҿ, and so from Ӟ ӟ.
- Build: nested components flattened, standard glyph names (uniXXXX), degenerate segments removed; name IDs 16
  and 17 are not used, as the family has one style.
Medeo Sans
- Descenders deepened from −150 to −198 units, Inter's depth: p q y j g ŋ þ ƴ ɲ ɖ μ ф ђ ӈ ԓ ԥ Ŋ Ƒ Ɲ Ӈ and, through
  components, р у ў ј ý ÿ ğ ģ and the rest. g keeps its overshoot (−210); ү ұ are at −198 in every weight.
- Lowercase Cyrillic tails ц д щ џ қ ң ҳ ҷ җ ҭ ӷ ӌ ҿ deepened to −150, level with the capital tails.
- Hooks ҕ ҧ ӄ Ӄ Ԓ at −198, level with р у ђ ӈ; the italic ӄ Ӄ hook is the upright hook slanted 12°.
- Capitals Ҕ Ҧ: the bowl lowered by 134 units to −150, the depth of the capital tails, as in the italic; the

- л Л from Inter, as their left leg was lighter than the stems in bold weights (88% of н in Bold and Black). Fitted
  to each master: legs and stems of н Н, bars of п П, Л as wide against П as before; the top of the hook of л is the
  top of the tail of а, the hook of Л keeps its height; sidebearings and kerning re-fitted to the old light of every

- Added ₿, ✓ and ★ from Inter 4.001, ✔, the Belarusian ruble sign at U+20C5 (the code Unicode 19.0 proposes), the

- Medeo Sans Display statics: the stylistic sets pointed to name IDs 266-276 instead of 256-266 (ss01 read
  "Rounded dot", ss02-ss11 had no name, as in RC4); they now read as in Medeo Sans. head flags bit 3 set, as in

1.000-rc4 (2026-10-28)
- Expanded Latin and Cyrillic repertoires and combining-mark support in Sans, Mono and Pixel.
  All three families cover 759 language alphabets in Google Fonts language data.
- Added Serbian and Macedonian localized italics, Pinyin and additional accent combinations.
- Revised Sans kerning at text and display optical sizes. Matching Latin and Cyrillic forms
  use consistent spacing; accented letters retain clearance for their marks.
- Restored the Geist upright a in Sans, including its accented and Cyrillic forms.
- Corrected Kazakh and extended Cyrillic descender joins. Pixel қ uses the Cyrillic к body
  in every shape; Pixel ĥ has additional clearance below its accent.
- Added variable-font vertical hinting to Sans and Mono, with coordinated base and accent alignment.
- Added static Sans, Sans Display, Mono and Pixel styles. Static Sans and Mono merge overlapping
  contours; variable fonts declare overlaps while preserving compatible outlines.
- Added Pixel tabular figures and currency signs; improved zero and stylistic-feature combinations.
- Preserved the SIL Open Font License and upstream Geist and Inter attribution.

1.000-rc3 (2026-10-25)
- Medeo Pixel keeps its pixels as components of one exported glyph, «pixel», instead of 26,023 decomposed
  contours. The TTF goes from 5.4 MB to 0.4 MB and its web files compress about 8× (WOFF) and 14× (WOFF2) instead
  of 52× and 110×, which Safari, every iPhone browser and the Instagram in-app browser rejected (and Chromium for
  WOFF2): Medeo Pixel now loads there. The drawing is the same; between ELSH 50 and 70, 3.6% of the points
  (46,926 of 1,287,954 at 60) move by 1–2 units of delta rounding.
- Nine ogonek letters (Ą Ę Į ą į ų and alternates) placed one pixel of the ogonek exactly on a pixel of the letter;
  that ogonek is now drawn with its own pixels and the duplicate is left out. Font Bakery no longer finds
  duplicate components.
- Medeo Sans and Medeo Mono change only their version.
